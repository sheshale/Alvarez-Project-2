A legacy of faith and education since 1927

Kabankalan Catholic College is a Christ-centered learning community serving Kabankalan City and the Diocese of Kabankalan.

  • Quality and inclusive Catholic education rooted in the Gospel.
  • Academic excellence grounded in faith, integrity, and service.
  • Basic education, Senior High School, and college programs for every stage of learning.
